Daily Health Guardian is an AI-powered health and wellness platform designed to help people build healthier habits and track their daily progress. The platform allows users to monitor essential health metrics such as water intake, sleep, physical activity, and personal wellness notes in a simple and user-friendly dashboard.

With intelligent insights, visual progress tracking, and personalized health recommendations, Daily Health Guardian helps users stay motivated and make informed lifestyle decisions. The platform focuses on preventive wellness by encouraging consistent healthy habits and providing easy access to health-related resources.

Key Features
Daily health tracking for sleep, hydration, and activity
AI-powered wellness insights and recommendations
Progress visualization with interactive reports
Secure and private health data management
User-friendly dashboard for habit building
Responsive design for desktop and mobile devices

Daily Health Guardian's mission is to empower individuals to take control of their well-being, improve daily habits, and achieve long-term health goals through technology and data-driven guidance.
